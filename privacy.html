Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

At Built by Oma, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us through our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you complete the contact form on our website to inquire about our web design services, we collect the following information:

  • Business Name: To identify your brand and check your current online presence.
  • Business Details: To understand your industry and specific design needs.
  • WhatsApp Number: To contact you directly for consultations, updates, and deliverables.
  • Email Address (Optional): To send email updates and formal project agreements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To communicate with you regarding your web design package inquiry.
  • To set up and custom-design your business website.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp for design reviews and project completion.

3. Data Protection and Sharing

Your privacy is important to us. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or any other commercial purposes. Your contact details are stored securely and are only accessed by members of the Built by Oma team working on your project.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website is a clean, static landing page. We do not use tracking cookies or collect behavioral statistics to run retargeting ads or monitor your activity.

5. Your Rights and Contact

You can request to view, edit, or delete any personal contact details we have collected for your project at any time. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us directly through our WhatsApp contact links.
