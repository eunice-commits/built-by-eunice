# Service Scope — Built by Eunice Business Launch Package

## Purpose
This document defines the scope of the ₦80,000 Business Launch Package.
It explains exactly what is included, what is not included, and how projects are delivered. This helps set clear expectations for both Built by Eunice and every client.

## Package Price
* Promotional Price: ₦80,000
* Regular Price: ₦150,000

The promotional price applies only to the first 10 business owners who complete the contact form during the promotional period.

## What's Included
Every Business Launch Package includes:
* Custom designed pages
* Mobile responsive design
* Basic SEO optimization
* Google Business Profile setup
* Free domain name for one year
* Free hosting for one year
* Free Meta Ads Starter Guide
* Contact form
* WhatsApp integration
* Business specific value resource where appropriate

## Standalone Support Benefit
* **One Week Post-Launch Support:** Highlighted separately from the deliverables checklist, this includes one week of post-launch technical and editing support.

## Standard Website Pages
Typical pages include:
* Home
* About
* Products or Services
* Testimonials
* Contact

*The page structure may vary depending on the business.*

## Business Specific Value Resource
Every website includes a valuable resource that keeps visitors engaged and encourages them to return.
Examples include:

### For a hair business:
* Natural hair care guide
* Hair maintenance tips
* Hair routine recommendations

### For a skincare brand:
* Skincare routine guide
* Ingredient education
* Skin type advice

### For a fashion business:
* Style tips
* Size guide
* Care instructions

### For a food business:
* Recipes
* Cooking tips
* Meal planning ideas

*The resource should be relevant to the client's business and provide genuine value to their customers.*

## Payment Terms
* A 70% deposit is required before work begins.
* The remaining 30% is due before the website is published.
* Work begins only after the initial payment has been confirmed.

## Project Timeline
* Most websites are completed within 5 to 10 business days after all required content has been received.
* Delays in receiving content or approvals may extend the timeline.

## Client Responsibilities
The client is responsible for providing:
* Business name
* Business description
* Logo
* Brand colors (if available)
* Product or service information
* Images
* Contact information
* Social media links
* Any required legal information
* Any additional content needed for the website

*Providing complete and accurate information helps ensure timely delivery.*

## Revisions
* The package includes up to two rounds of revisions after the initial design is presented.
* Revisions cover adjustments to the agreed design and content.
* Requests for additional design concepts or major changes after approval may require a separate quotation.

## Post Launch Support
One week of post launch support is included.
Support covers:
* Minor content corrections
* Bug fixes
* Guidance on using the website
* Technical troubleshooting related to the delivered website

*Support does not include redesigns or adding new features.*

## What's Not Included
The package does not include:
* Logo design
* Brand identity design
* Copywriting from scratch
* Professional photography
* Video production
* Ongoing website management
* Monthly SEO services
* Social media management
* Paid advertising management
* Email marketing setup
* Custom software development
* Membership systems
* Advanced booking platforms
* Multi vendor marketplaces
* Custom dashboards
* Mobile app development
* Unlimited revisions

*These services may be quoted separately if required.*

## Domain and Hosting Renewal
* The domain name and hosting are included for the first year.
* Renewals after the first year are the responsibility of the client.
* Renewal reminders can be provided before the expiration date.

## Ownership
* After final payment has been received, the client owns the website content and the licensed assets used for their project.
* Built by Eunice reserves the right to display the completed website in its portfolio unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## Communication
* Project communication will take place primarily through WhatsApp and email.
* Clients are encouraged to respond promptly to questions and review requests to avoid delays.

## Quality Commitment
Every website is designed to be:
* Professional
* Mobile responsive
* Easy to navigate
* Fast loading
* Search engine optimized
* Aligned with the client's business goals

Built by Eunice considers a project complete only when the website is live, functioning correctly, and the agreed scope has been delivered.

## Scope Changes
* Any request outside the scope of this package will be reviewed separately.
* Additional features or services will be quoted before work begins.
* No extra work will be carried out without the client's approval.

## Service Principles
* Every project is delivered with transparency, professionalism, and attention to detail.
* The goal is not simply to create a website but to provide a professional online presence that helps businesses build trust, attract customers, and grow with confidence.
