# Built by Eunice Landing Page Context

## Project Overview
This project is a high converting sales page for Built by Eunice, a website design team founded by Eunice Orji.
The purpose of this page is to convert business owners into qualified leads for the Business Launch Package through a simple contact form that continues the sales conversation on WhatsApp.
This page is not a portfolio website. It is not a company website. It is a focused sales page with one primary goal: generate qualified leads.

## About Built by Eunice
Built by Eunice is a website design team that helps business owners establish a professional online presence.
The brand combines website design with business strategy to help clients build credibility, increase visibility, and make it easier for customers to find and trust their businesses online.
Every website is designed with the business owner's goals in mind, not just visual appeal.
The emphasis is on creating websites that are simple, professional, mobile friendly, search engine optimized, and built to support business growth.

## Project Goal
The objective of this landing page is to persuade business owners to request a website through the Business Launch Package.
Visitors should quickly understand:
* What is being offered
* Why they need it
* Why Built by Eunice is the right choice
* What the package includes
* How the process works
* What they should do next

Every section should move visitors naturally toward completing the contact form.

## Target Audience
The primary audience includes:
* Small business owners
* Fashion brands
* Beauty businesses
* Hair vendors
* Skincare brands
* Restaurants and food businesses
* Service providers
* Coaches and consultants
* Personal brands
* Startups

Most visitors are not highly technical. They care more about business results than technical features.
The copy should explain benefits in simple language without unnecessary technical jargon.

## Core Problem
Many businesses rely entirely on social media platforms.
While social media is valuable, it does not provide the credibility, discoverability, or control that a professional website offers.
Without a website, businesses risk:
* Losing customers to competitors
* Looking less credible
* Missing Google search opportunities
* Making it harder for customers to learn about their products or services
* Depending entirely on changing social media algorithms

The landing page should position a website as a business investment rather than an expense.

## The Business Launch Package
The Business Launch Package includes:
* Professionally designed website
* Up to five professionally designed pages
* Mobile responsive design
* SEO optimization
* Google Business Profile setup
* Free domain name for one year
* Free hosting for one year
* Free Meta Ads Starter Guide
* Contact form
* WhatsApp integration

### Standalone Benefit: Post-Launch Support
A standalone support card/callout highlighting 1 week of post-launch technical and editing support is placed directly beneath the package deliverables list to make it stand out.

The regular package value is ₦150,000.
The promotional price is ₦80,000.
The first 10 business owners who complete the contact form during the promotional week qualify for the ₦80,000 price.

## Payment Structure
Clients pay:
* 70% before work begins
* 30% before the website goes live

No payment is collected directly from the landing page.
All sales conversations continue on WhatsApp after the contact form is submitted.

## User Journey
1. Visitor arrives from:
   * TikTok
   * Instagram
   * Facebook
   * LinkedIn
   * Google Search
   * Influencer promotions
   * Direct referrals
2. Visitor reads the offer.
3. Visitor understands the value.
4. Visitor builds trust through projects and testimonials.
5. Visitor completes the contact form.
6. Visitor is redirected to WhatsApp with a prefilled message.
7. The Built by Eunice team continues the conversation.

## Secondary Offer
Some visitors may prefer learning to build their own website.
For those visitors, a second option is available.

### Learn to Build Your Own Website
* This is a recorded course priced at ₦10,000.

* This option should be presented as an alternative path, not as a cheaper version of the main service.

## Brand Personality
Built by Eunice should feel:
* Professional
* Strategic
* Honest
* Friendly
* Modern
* Reliable
* Approachable

The brand should never feel overly corporate or overly casual.

## Brand Voice
The copy should be:
* Direct
* Human
* Conversational
* Clear
* Confident

Avoid:
* Corporate jargon
* Fake urgency
* Exaggerated marketing claims
* Technical language that confuses business owners

Every sentence should help visitors understand the value of the offer.

## Trust Principles
Trust should be built through:
* Real client testimonials
* Real website projects
* Clear pricing
* Transparent process
* Team introduction
* Honest communication

*Never create fake scarcity or fake testimonials.*

## Conversion Strategy
The page should guide visitors through this progression:
1. Problem
2. Solution (Business Launch Package)
3. Pricing and Offer
4. How It Works
5. Recent Projects (Proof)
6. Testimonials (Proof)
7. About the Team
8. Choose Your Path
9. Frequently Asked Questions
10. Contact Form
11. WhatsApp Conversation

Every section should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence.

## Success Metrics
The page is successful when visitors:
* Stay engaged throughout the page
* Click the primary call to action
* Complete the contact form
* Continue the conversation on WhatsApp
* Become paying clients
